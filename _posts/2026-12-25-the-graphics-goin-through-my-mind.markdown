It's a failing of mine to continually redesign my things. Trying to give form and style to a website, or a brand that has no clients and even less content. It's difficult to try to make something out of less than nothing, or something sparse. But there is an advantage. You can change directions and play a lot, even if most of the things you're playing with don't get shipped or built. 

I've begun to think that it might be useful/enjoyable/interesting to show that thinking process. The creative struggle can be instructive to not just what I'm thinking about and the problems I'm trying to solve or don't have, but what gets left behind in the creative process. And iteration that takes place to get things rollin.

In that vein I'm gonna dump all the inspiration that has me thinkin'.
